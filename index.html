Dress Code

Attire Guideline

Semi-Formal

衣著要求：敬請穿著優雅合宜之服裝

男士們請穿著有袖上衣及不露趾的鞋子
女士們請穿著優雅得體的正裝

請勿穿著拖鞋入場

Formal Attire Required: We kindly request our guests to dress in elegant, appropriate attire.

Gentlemen are requested to wear sleeved shirts and closed-toe shoes.
Ladies are requested to wear semi-formal attire.

Please note that slippers are not permitted within the venue.
