致一路相伴的你

To our dearest ones

「把自己活成一道光，因為你不知道，誰會藉著你的光，走出了黑暗。」

— 泰戈爾

"Become a light; you never know who might find their way out of the darkness because of yours."

— Rabindranath Tagore

謝謝你，出現在我們生命裡。正因為有你的陪伴與照拂，我們才成為了現在的模樣。而我與睿茂決定在未來攜手共度的餘生，成為彼此的光。

我們將舉辦一場溫馨簡單的婚禮，捨去繁瑣的儀式，只想在這個特別的日子，把最愛的人聚在一起吃頓好吃的、聊聊近況，並跟大家一起分享幸福的喜悅。若能有你的笑聲相伴，一定會變得更加溫暖完整。

期待在那天，與你相遇。

Thank you for being such a meaningful part of our lives. It is because of your companionship and support that we have become who we are today. Now, Ruei-Mao and I have decided to share the rest of our lives together, becoming each other's light.

We are planning a small, intimate wedding—stripping away the traditional formalities. We simply want to gather our favorite people on this special day to enjoy a great meal, catch up on life, and share the joy of our journey with you all.

Our day would be so much warmer and more complete with your laughter joining ours.

We cannot wait to celebrate with you.

馨瑩 & 睿茂 敬上

With love, Sami & Milo

Forever Begins Today

Milo 莊睿茂
&
Sami 曾馨瑩
